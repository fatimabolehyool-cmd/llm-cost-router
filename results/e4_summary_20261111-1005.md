**The senior is not ground truth:** Opus 5.5 disagreed with the gold label on 26 of 200 questions (banking77-1002, banking77-1328, banking77-1482, banking77-2172, banking77-2209, banking77-2299, banking77-2495, banking77-2561, banking77-2600, banking77-2617, banking77-2633, banking77-271 …). These count as senior errors; senior answers never replace gold labels (label provenance, architecture section E).

Limitations: one run per model (Opus 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 accept no temperature setting, so repeated runs can differ); 200 questions give wide intervals; the junior cost is local hardware and electricity, not counted as paid cost; these 200 questions were also used to choose the junior pair (E1), which can flatter the juniors.
