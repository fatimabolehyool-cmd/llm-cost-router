# ADR-7: Junior pair gemma3:12b and phi4:14b, chosen by correlated error

**Status:** Accepted, implemented. **Date:** 24 Oct 2026 (architecture deliverable).

## Decision

The juniors are gemma3:12b and phi4:14b, chosen by false agreement and correlated error rather than single-model accuracy.

## Alternatives considered

Highest single accuracy; 3-4B models.

## Reason and evidence

E1: false agreement 21.7% against 31.7% for the best small pair; models from two different labs.
