# ADR-27: Target customer: the help desk of a UAE bank

**Status:** Accepted. **Date:** 24 Oct 2026 (architecture deliverable).

## Decision

The target customer is the customer-support help desk of a UAE bank.

## Alternatives considered

A general-purpose router; an IT help desk.

## Reason and evidence

A named customer makes the value proposition concrete; it matches Banking77 and the PII detector (UAE phones, Emirates IDs, cards) and makes the targets testable.

## As built

Arabic messages are rejected (ADR-21), which is a gap for this customer and a stated limitation.
